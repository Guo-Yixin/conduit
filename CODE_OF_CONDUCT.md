# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Conduit contributors and maintainers are expected to make participation
welcoming, respectful, and constructive. Disagreement about design and code is
normal; harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, intimidation, and the
publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Scope

This standard applies in the repository, issue tracker, pull requests, project
discussions, and other spaces where someone is representing the Conduit
community.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to `joshua.owens@gmail.com`. Maintainers
may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when behavior is inconsistent with this standard. Reports will be
handled as confidentially as practical.
